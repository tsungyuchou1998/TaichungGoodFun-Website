ABOUT TAICHUNG

漫步臺中，
讓時光慢慢流動。

臺中，一座融合歷史、人文與創新的城市。從百年古蹟、特色聚落到現代地標，串聯出豐富的文化軌跡；結合在地美食、自然景觀與職人精神，邀您一起發掘屬於臺中的精彩故事與城市魅力。

Taichung is where history, culture, and creativity meet. Wander through century-old landmarks, charming neighborhoods, and modern cityscapes. Savor local flavors, discover artisan craftsmanship, and escape into rolling hills and coastal wetlands. With stories hidden around every corner, Taichung invites you to slow down, explore, and uncover its quiet yet captivating charm.
